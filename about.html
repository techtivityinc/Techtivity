Techtivity is a small, deliberately independent studio. We build products, systems and interfaces for teams who'd rather move carefully than move fast.

Our story

We started Techtivity in 2021 with one belief: that most software is built faster than it is understood.

Since then we've partnered with founders, research labs and product teams to slow down just enough to build things that last — infrastructure, spatial tools, intelligence layers — without losing the urgency that got them started.

Today we're a team of fourteen, working across Paris, Lisbon and remotely, on a small number of long relationships rather than a large number of short ones. See selected work ↗

In brief
A studio-sized team that behaves like an in-house one: we embed with the people who'll live with what we build, favor fewer relationships held for longer, and treat every technical or visual decision as something that has to earn its place before it ships.
