01

Four people, one broken job board, a weekend.

[ BUILD NIGHT, THURS 7PM ]

Techtivity started in 2023 when four sophomores got tired of complaining about the campus job board and rebuilt it in a weekend. It's since grown into a hundred-plus student engineers, designers, and researchers who'd rather ship the thing than write the essay about it. We meet Thursdays, build in public, and open-source everything that isn't embarrassing yet.

[ 01 ]

Build in the open

Every project starts as a public repo, issue tracker included, from commit one.

[ 02 ]

Ship before it's ready

We'd rather learn from a rough version in someone's hands than polish something nobody's used.

[ 03 ]

Teach what you just learned

Every member runs at least one workshop a year, on whatever they figured out last month.

2021 Founded over a hackathon weekend by four CS sophomores.
2022 First open build night — 40 people, one borrowed classroom.
2023 Shipped Pulse to 2,000+ students across two campuses.
2024 Ran our first Summit — 12 workshops, one very long day.
